How scores are calculated

Your score is out of 100 points and is built entirely from how regularly you commit — not how much code you write, and not your total commit count.

1. Only your active days count

Multiple commits on the same day count as one "active day." What matters is the gap, in days, between one active day and the next.

2. Each gap earns points on a sliding scale

Gap between commitsPoints earned
≤ 7 daysFull points (11.1)
8–10 daysPartial points (4.4)
11+ daysZero points

Those points add up across all your gaps, up to a maximum of 100 — that's what "100 pts over 90 days" means: committing roughly weekly for the whole window earns a perfect score.

3. Staying silent right now costs you — gradually

The gaps above only describe your past commits. They can't see that you might be silent right now. So your score also tapers down the longer it's been since your last commit:

  • 0–7 days since your last commit: no penalty at all.
  • 8–21 days: your score eases downward, a little more each day.
  • 21+ days: your score is held at a 20-point floor.
